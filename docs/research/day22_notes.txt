Day 22 - Architecture Selection

Option 1: CNN
Example: ResNet50

Pros:
- Good for images
- Fast
- Easy to train

Cons:
- Less powerful than transformers

Option 2: Transformer

Pros:
- State of the art
- Learns long-range relationships

Cons:
- Needs lots of data
- Slow

Option 3: Hybrid (CNN + Transformer)

Pros:
- Best of both worlds

Cons:
- More complex

Chosen Architecture: CNN

Reason:
- Works well with images
- Suitable for small datasets
- Faster than Transformers
- Good for GTX 1650 GPU

Backbone: ResNet-50

Reason:
- Popular computer vision model
- Good feature extraction
- Easy to use with PyTorch
- Well documented

Decision Head: MLP

Outputs:
1. Steering
2. Throttle
3. Brake

Flow:

Camera Image
    ↓
ResNet-50
    ↓
Feature Vector
    ↓
MLP
    ↓
Steering
Throttle
Brake
